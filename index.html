Color Sort Rush Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Fresco Vida · mofarjul619@gmail.com

Color Sort Rush (“the game”) is a puzzle game for Android made by Fresco Vida (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is processed when you play, who processes it and the choices you have. The game does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers and do not receive personal information about you; the online services described below are provided by Google.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, unlocked items, settings and statistics are saved on your device so the game works offline. We do not receive this information.

If Android Backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup with Google, so your progress can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new device. Google's backup is governed by your Google account settings and Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • information about your interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app's and the SDK's performance.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are personalized. You can review them at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy Choices (shown where it applies). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and watching an ad is never required to play.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

You can choose to use Google Play Games services for worldwide leaderboards and achievements. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and gamer name), the scores the game submits to leaderboards and the achievements you unlock. The game shows your Play Games name on its profile badge. You can play without signing in. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Play Games app or your Google Account.

4. Leaderboard scores

Scores sent to leaderboards are checked for plausibility on your device first. Leaderboards are public: other players can see your gamer name and scores on them, as set by your Play Games profile visibility.

5. Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data about them was collected, contact us and we will help you have it deleted.

6. Security and international transfers

All information sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google may process it in countries other than yours, under its own safeguards.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • Review ad privacy choices in Settings → Privacy (where it applies), and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Play without signing in to Google Play Games, or delete your Play Games data in your Google Account.
  • Uninstall the game to delete the data stored on your device.
  • For questions or data requests, email mofarjul619@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the game's store listing.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: mofarjul619@gmail.com, Fresco Vida.
